# Refund Policy

*Last updated: November 22, 2026*

## 1. Try the Free Edition First

The full **Yearning v4 Community Edition** is free and open-source (`AGPL-3.0-or-later`). Please test it against your PostgreSQL metadata database, target engines, and infrastructure before buying **Yearning v4 Pro**.

## 2. Digital License Policy (Non-Refundable)

Because you can evaluate Yearning freely beforehand and Pro is delivered digitally via an offline Ed25519-signed license key, **all completed purchases are final and non-refundable once your license key is issued**.

The only exceptions are:

- **Duplicate charges or billing errors** (for example, accidentally paying twice for the same order); or
- **Where required by law or Creem's MoR rules** (such as mandatory consumer withdrawal rights that weren't waived, or Merchant of Record compliance requirements).

## 3. Canceling a Subscription

- You can cancel an **Annual Subscription (`$599 / year`)** anytime before your next renewal date in the Creem billing portal.
- You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current paid period. Once a billing period starts, we don't offer partial or prorated refunds (outside billing errors or mandatory legal exceptions).

## 4. If an Exception Refund Is Issued

When a refund is approved, the corresponding license key is deactivated, and you agree to stop using the Pro modules (your cluster continues working normally on the free Community Edition).

## 5. Need Help With a Charge?

Please email **`henry@yearning.io`** with your Creem order ID before opening a card dispute or chargeback—we respond quickly and are happy to sort out billing mistakes directly. Approved exception refunds go back to your original payment method via **Creem** within **5–10 business days**.
